Installing on macOS?

The Mac builds are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized, so macOS may block the first launch. After trying to open VideoLUA, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway to authorize an app you trust. You do not need to disable Gatekeeper. The macOS packages include .NET, FFmpeg, and FFprobe.
